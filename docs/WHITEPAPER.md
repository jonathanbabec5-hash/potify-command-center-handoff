# Potify Command Center
## Whitepaper · public developer edition

**November 25, 2026 · Public specification · original source pending**

### Goal and audience

Identify the original command-center system and the interfaces that can safely be shared. The immediate audience is founder and collaborating workspace maintainer.

### Design and implementation

The intended operator workspace has its own source-recovery checklist. This edition publishes a specification for identifying its inputs, outputs, owners and acceptance test. Treat collaborator access and public distribution scope as separate facts. Identify the original owner and compare the workspace's purpose to the other two operations tools.

### What a contributor can establish

A Replit workspace listing was located. No source export or executable was available to test. A title in Replit proves a workspace exists. It does not establish authorship, current functionality, or how its internals should be published.

### Evidence and outstanding work

Only this newly authored specification is being released. Original workspace code, operating records and redistributable scope remain unverified. The next acceptance case is concrete. Obtain a source-only export, identify its remote and license terms, and run its own documented startup command.

### Place in POTIFY

Validate one real operator problem before proposing a paid deployment or support offer. The marketing plan gives the test window and the evidence for a commercial outcome.
