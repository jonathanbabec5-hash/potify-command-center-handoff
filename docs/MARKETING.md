# Potify Command Center marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance coordinates the handoff with the original workspace maintainer.

**Audience.** Founder and collaborating workspace maintainer. **Offer.** Identify the original command-center system and the interfaces that can safely be shared.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |

### Sales test

Publicly share this specification. Promote a working product only after source and scope are established. The first commercial milestone is an operator interview, with a paid pilot contingent on release rights. Validate one real operator problem before proposing a paid deployment or support offer.

### Measurement and decision

Use `command-center-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** Only this newly authored specification is being released. Original workspace code, operating records and redistributable scope remain unverified. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
